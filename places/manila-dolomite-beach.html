Manila Dolomite Beach

Manila Baywalk Dolomite Beach is a man-made beach along the shores of Manila Bay that offers a relaxing view of the sea. It is especially popular for watching the sunset, although visitors should take note that it closes at 6:00 PM. While the smell of the sea may sometimes be unpleasant, the beautiful scenery makes the visit worthwhile. It is also a nice place to relax, spend time with friends, and have meaningful conversations. Eating foods are prohibited on the premises.
