National Museum

The National Museum of the Philippines is a great place to visit for anyone interested in Philippine history, culture, and art. Aside from exploring the museum, visitors can relax on the benches outside and enjoy the beautiful sunset. It is especially enjoyable during cool and clear days, although the heat or rain can make staying outside uncomfortable. Nearby, you can also find vendors selling street food such as tuhog-tuhog and ice cream.
