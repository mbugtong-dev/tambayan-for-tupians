Arroceros Forest Park

Arroceros Forest Park is a lush green space in Manila that provides a refreshing escape from the busy urban environment. The park is a great place to relax while appreciating the variety of plants, trees, and other natural scenery. I especially enjoy spending time there because simply observing and capturing the beauty of nature can feel peaceful. It is a place where visitors can slow down, breathe, and appreciate the quiet side of the city.

Location Opening Hours Entrance Fee Best Time to Visit
Antonio Villegas St., Ermita, Manila (near Central LRT Station) 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM Free Morning or late afternoon
