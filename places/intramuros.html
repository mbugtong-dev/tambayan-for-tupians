Intramuros (Public Spaces)

Intramuros is a historic walled area in Manila known for its old architecture, cobblestone streets, and important historical landmarks. Visitors can explore places such as Fort Santiago and San Agustin Church while learning more about Philippine history. Aside from its historical value, it is also a great place to walk around, relax, and create memories with friends. Despite being surrounded by the busy city, Intramuros has a uniquely calm and mysterious atmosphere.
